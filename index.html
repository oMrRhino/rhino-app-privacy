Workshop Pal

Privacy policy · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Your projects stay on your phone.

Workshop Pal works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, app-operated servers or automatic project uploads. You choose when and where to export files.

Who provides Workshop Pal

Workshop Pal is an Android woodworking and cutting-plan application provided by Rhino App Development. For privacy questions, support or a request relating to information you have sent us, contact rhinoappdevelopment@protonmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter or generate for your projects. This can include project and material names, dimensions, quantities, units, fitting gaps, design settings, shopping items, fixing specifications, notes, purchase ticks and modification dates. Local project and piece identifiers allow the app to keep these records together.

This information is processed locally to calculate cutting layouts, create shopping lists and build packs, and reopen saved projects. The app does not send these records or identifiers to Rhino App Development. It requests no internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics or remote crash-reporting SDK.

Files you choose to import or export

You can export PDFs, text shopping lists and editable JSON backups, and import a backup you select. Exports can contain the project names, notes and other project information you entered. Backup restoration replaces the current project list only after confirmation.

Android's system document picker controls the file and destination you choose. Selecting a cloud-storage provider may transfer the selected file through that provider under its own privacy terms. Workshop Pal does not automatically select a cloud provider, initiate project synchronisation or control external copies. Import access is limited to the document you select.

Storage, security and deletion

Saved app records use Android's private application storage and the device's security protections. The app's configuration excludes automatic Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its app data. Use an exported JSON backup if you want to retain editable projects when changing phones.

Local records remain until you delete a project, replace the records by restoring a backup, clear app storage or uninstall the app. There is no remote recovery service or app account to delete.

Exported files are separate from app storage and are not encrypted by Workshop Pal. Deleting a project or uninstalling the app does not remove exported copies. Manage or delete those copies through the location or provider you selected.

Purchases and Android services

Google Play handles app purchases. Workshop Pal does not receive payment-card details and has no in-app checkout or subscription service. Google Play, Android and any file provider you choose may process information under their own policies; those services are not operated by Rhino App Development.

When you contact support

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you send. We use that information to answer your enquiry, resolve the reported issue or handle a privacy request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or project files that are unnecessary for your enquiry.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to handle the enquiry and any necessary business or legal record, then deleted when no longer required. You can request deletion by emailing the contact above; we may retain information where a legal obligation requires it. Email delivery and storage use the email providers involved in the correspondence. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them.

Intended audience and policy changes

Workshop Pal is intended for adult DIYers and woodworkers. If the app's information handling changes, we will update this policy and its date. The app includes privacy text that can be read offline.
